About

I'm Brandan, a game designer based in New Jersey.

Before game design I spent 7+ years as a direct response copywriter, writing ads, emails and landing pages for ecommerce and software brands. That work taught me to test everything, cut what doesn't work and always think about what the player (or reader) actually sees and feels.

I bring the same habits to games. I design levels and encounters, script them in C# in Unity, play them over and over, and change whatever feels wrong. Escape from Morvath is my first finished game, from the first sketch to the final boss.

What I do

  • Level design: layouts, pacing, gating and player guidance
  • Encounter and boss design: phases, attacks, tells and difficulty
  • Gameplay scripting and prototyping in Unity with C#
  • Clear design documents that a team can build from
