About

I'm Brandan, a game designer based in New Jersey and open to relocating.

I'm a game designer focused on levels, encounters and boss fights. My favorite part is getting the space, the enemies and the player's abilities to work together, so every fight feels made for the place it happens in.

I studied programming at RRC Polytech, so I can prototype my own ideas and work easily with engineers.

Before games, I spent 7+ years as a direct response copywriter, where every ad lived or died on real results. I bring that same habit to game design: watch what players actually do, find where they get stuck or lose interest, and fix it.
