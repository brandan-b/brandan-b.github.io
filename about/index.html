About

I'm Brandan, a game designer based in New Jersey and open to relocating.

Before game design I spent 7+ years as a direct response copywriter, writing ads, emails and landing pages for ecommerce and software brands. That work taught me to test everything, cut what doesn't work and always think about what the player (or reader) actually sees and feels.

I also studied full stack web development at RRC Polytech in Winnipeg, so I'm comfortable working in code. I script my own gameplay, build my own tools and can talk the same language as the engineers I work with.

I bring all of that to games. I design levels and encounters, build them in Unity and Unreal Engine 5, play them over and over, and change whatever feels wrong. Escape from Morvath is my first finished game, from the first sketch to the final boss.

What I do

  • Level design: layouts, pacing, gating and player guidance
  • Encounter and boss design: phases, attacks, tells and difficulty
  • Gameplay scripting and prototyping
  • Clear design documents a team can build from

Tools

  • Unity and C#
  • Unreal Engine 5 and Blueprints
  • Java, JavaScript, HTML and CSS
  • Git and GitHub
