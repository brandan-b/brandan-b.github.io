Escape from Morvath

A 2D dungeon crawler made in Unity. You play a young dragon whelp who has to collect three seals and escape the lair of Dragon Lord Morvath.

How the game flows

  • The Hatchery: the tutorial, where you learn to move, fight and cast.
  • The Sigil Halls: the hub that connects every area.
  • The Ritual Chamber and The Dragon Crypt: one seal each, played in either order.
  • The Crucible: the third seal, which opens once you have the first two.
  • The Throne Room: the fight against Morvath, which needs all three seals.

The Morvath fight

The final boss has four parts. First you hold off waves of whelps. Then Morvath fights in human form and throws soulfire orbs. Next he transforms and heals, and finally he fights as a dragon. In the dragon phase you light four soulfire braziers while he uses Deep Breath, swoops across the room and calls in new whelps every 25s. Once three braziers are lit, the whelps stop coming.

Design choices

  • Never mislead the player. I hid the Soulfire timer in the dragon phase, because seeing it made one brazier look like enough.
  • Keep the boss on screen during big attacks. Deep Breath is a fly-by right over the player, so you always see it coming.
  • A stuck player should never have to restart. The pause menu has an Unstuck button with a cooldown.
  • Test it in game, not on paper. I tried 8-direction art for the dragon and cut it because it felt clunky to play. Four directions read better.
  • Let players set their own keys. The Keybinds screen gives every action a main and a backup key.
